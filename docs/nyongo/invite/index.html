How to join

  1. Install Yonalar on your phone.
  2. Create your account.
  3. Open Profile → Invitation code and enter the code above.

Your accounts are then linked. What they recorded reaches you as requests: you accept them, propose a change, or decline. Nothing is added to your account without your agreement.
