# Privacy policy
Version 2026-11-07. Draft for testing, subject to legal review.

## Who we are
Yonalar is operated by Gigayar. Contact: support@gigayar.com

## What we collect
- Your account: name, username, email address, phone number (optional) and password. Your password is stored in a form that nobody can read, not even us.
- What you record: debts, payments, due dates, notes, the contacts you add (a name, and optionally their email or phone number), messages, reminder settings and their history, and requests exchanged with other users.
- Your gifts: money you note that you gave or received (amount, currency, date, person, occasion, note), and your giving limit if you set one.
- The cotisations and tontines you organize or join: their settings, their members, what each person says they gave or paid (and how: cash or mobile money), the organizer's confirmations, the rounds and the pots handed over, departures and their reason if you give one, and the messages in their chat.
- Your profile photo or avatar, if you choose one (it is optional). The photo is kept small and without any hidden information: no place, no date, no device.
- Your reports and the people you block.
- When you last used the app, which is used for read receipts, for showing you as "online" and to know whether a reminder reached you.
- Technical data: a notification token for your phone, the language of the app, and basic server logs (such as IP address and time of requests) kept for security.

We do not access your phone's address book or your location, and we never ask for bank or card details. The camera and your photos are only used if you choose to add a profile photo, and only for the photo you choose.

## Why we use it
- To run Yonalar: show your debts, share a debt with the other person when they accept it, and deliver reminders and notifications.
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
- To answer you when you contact support.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who can see what
- Your private records (debts with your contacts) are visible only to you.
- Someone on Yonalar who types your exact username or email address can find you, to send you a request or an invitation. If they find you by your username, they see neither your email nor your phone number. Your email and phone number are shown only to people you have an accepted debt or a group with, and to the people you send a request to.
- A shared debt, its payments and its chat are visible to you and to the other person on it.
- People you add as contacts are not told about it, unless you choose to send them email reminders or an invitation (you send it yourself, from your phone).
- Your gifts are visible only to you. The other person is never told.
- Your profile photo is visible only to people you have an accepted debt or a group with, and to the person you send a request or an invitation to; never to someone you blocked or who blocked you. Your avatar shows wherever your name does.
- In a cotisation, members see what the organizer chose: only the total, who gave, or who gave how much. This also applies to a gift you declared that the organizer hasn't confirmed yet. The organizer sees everything. In a tontine, every member sees who paid in each round.
- A group's chat is visible to its members. Why someone leaves a tontine is seen only by the organizer and that person.
- People you share a debt, a contact or a group with can see whether you are online or when you last were, unless you turn off "Show when I'm online" in Profile (then you no longer see theirs either).
- Your payment record is shown to others only if you choose to share it, and only as counts, never amounts or names.
- When you report someone, your report (and the reported message, if any) is emailed to our support team, who review it.

## AI assistants (if you connect one)
- You can connect your own assistant (for example Claude or ChatGPT) to your account. Nothing is connected unless you do it yourself.
- You choose what it may read: your loans and debts, your tontines and cotisations, your gifts. If you allow it, it can also note gifts for you (they stay private and show that it noted them) and prepare a loan, a debt, a payment or a reminder: nothing is sent or recorded until you have checked and confirmed it yourself in Yonalar. It never accepts, confirms or deletes anything on your behalf.
- What you ask it and what Yonalar answers it, including your contacts' names and the amounts, is passed to the company that runs that assistant, under its own terms and privacy policy.
- Yonalar never gives it your password. In Profile, AI assistants, you see each connected assistant and what it read or did, and you can disconnect it at any time. Changing your password disconnects them all.

## Services we rely on
- Hetzner: hosts our servers, in the European Union.
- Google Firebase: delivers notifications to your phone.
- Resend: sends emails (reminders, password reset, email verification).

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union, with appropriate safeguards.

## How long we keep your data
As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account, your private records, your contacts, your gifts, your messages (including in group chats), your profile photo and your connected assistants are deleted. Your photo is also deleted as soon as you remove it. When you change your username or delete your account, the old name stays reserved for 30 days, so nobody else takes it right away, and is then released. On your phone, Yonalar keeps a copy of what you have opened, so you can read it without a network; it is deleted when you sign out or delete your account. Debts you shared with other people stay in their records as their own private notes, because they are part of their history too.

In cotisations and tontines, what you gave or paid stays in the group's history, under your name, because it is part of the other members' accounts. A group you organize passes to another member.

## Your rights
You can access, correct and delete your data. Most of it can be changed directly in the app, and you can delete your account in Profile, Delete my account. For any other request, write to support@gigayar.com. You can also contact your data protection authority.

## Children
Yonalar is not intended for people under 18.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the app will ask you to read and accept the new version.
