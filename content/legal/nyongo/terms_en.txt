# Terms of use
Version 2026-10-13. Draft for testing, subject to legal review.

## What Nyongo is
Nyongo is a personal tool to keep track of money lent and borrowed between people, and to send reminders about it. Nyongo is operated by Gigayar.

## What Nyongo is not
- Nyongo does not move, hold or transfer money. Payments happen outside the app.
- Nyongo is not a lender, a bank, a payment service or a debt collection agency.
- Nyongo does not verify what users record. A debt, payment or message in Nyongo is a statement made by a user, not proof that it is true.
- Records in Nyongo are not legal documents.

## Your account
- You must be at least 18 years old to use Nyongo.
- Keep your password private. You are responsible for what is done with your account.
- The information you give about yourself must be truthful.

## What you record
- You are responsible for the debts, payments, contacts and messages you enter.
- Only record debts that really exist. A debt with another Nyongo user is shared with them only if they accept it.
- If someone declines a debt, respect their answer. You may keep it as a private note, but Nyongo will not send them reminders about it.

## Reminders and messages
- Use reminders and chat respectfully. Harassment, threats, insults and misleading content are not allowed.
- We may suspend or delete accounts that abuse other people.

## Disagreements between users
Nyongo does not settle disagreements between users and is not responsible for whether a debt is repaid. Please resolve disagreements directly with the other person, or through the appropriate authorities.

## Availability
We do our best to keep Nyongo working, but we cannot guarantee that it will always be available or free of errors. Features may change over time.

## Deleting your account
You can delete your account at any time in Profile, Delete my account. The Privacy policy explains what happens to your data.

## Changes to these terms
If these terms change, the app will ask you to read and accept the new version.
