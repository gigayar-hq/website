# Terms of use
Version 2026-11-07. Draft for testing, subject to legal review.

## What Yonalar is
Yonalar is a personal tool to keep track of money lent and borrowed between people, and to send reminders about it. Yonalar is operated by Gigayar.

## What Yonalar is not
- Yonalar does not move, hold or transfer money. Payments happen outside the app.
- Yonalar is not a lender, a bank, a payment service or a debt collection agency.
- Yonalar does not verify what users record. A debt, payment or message in Yonalar is a statement made by a user, not proof that it is true.
- Records in Yonalar are not legal documents.

## Your account
- You must be at least 18 years old to use Yonalar.
- Keep your password private. You are responsible for what is done with your account.
- The information you give about yourself must be truthful.
- A profile photo is optional. It must show you or stay neutral: no offensive image and no one else's photo. A reported photo may be removed.
- Your username must not pass for someone else or for Yonalar. A misleading username that is reported may be changed.

## What you record
- You are responsible for the debts, payments, contacts and messages you enter.
- Only record debts that really exist. A debt with another Yonalar user is shared with them only if they accept it.
- If someone declines a debt, respect their answer. You may keep it as a private note, but Yonalar will not send them reminders about it.

## Reminders and messages
- Reminders only go out if you turn them on. You choose who receives them, how often and how (notification, email); Yonalar sends them on your behalf, without checking that the debt is real or that the person agrees to be reminded.
- You alone are responsible for the reminders, requests, invitations and messages you send through Yonalar, and for their consequences, including if the person complains of harassment, files a complaint or takes legal action against you. Yonalar is not responsible for them.
- Only send reminders for a real debt, to the person who owes it, at a reasonable rhythm. Stop them when the debt is settled or if the person asks you to. You can pause or stop them at any time.
- If a claim is made against Yonalar because of reminders or messages you chose to send, you bear its consequences, to the extent the law allows.
- The first time you turn on reminders, you confirm in the app that you accept this responsibility; we keep the date of that agreement.
- Use reminders and chat respectfully. Harassment, threats, insults and misleading content are not allowed.
- We may suspend or delete accounts that abuse other people.

## Cotisations and tontines
- Yonalar does not collect, hold or pay out a group's money. Gifts and payments are made in cash or by mobile money, outside the app.
- The organizer is responsible for the money they receive and for what they confirm. Yonalar keeps a record of what members declare and what the organizer confirms, without checking it.
- Yonalar is not responsible if a member or an organizer doesn't pay, doesn't hand over a pot, or leaves the group.

What it prepares for you (a loan, a debt, a payment, a reminder) is sent or recorded only if you do it yourself, and you are responsible for it like anything you record; a gift it notes for you counts as noted by you. Using it is governed by the terms of the company that runs it.
